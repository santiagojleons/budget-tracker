How This Budget Works

This app uses a percentage-based budgeting method — your monthly income is divided into four buckets so every dollar has a purpose. Here's the default breakdown:

50%
Essentials
Rent, groceries, utilities, transport — the non-negotiables.
15%
Growth
Investing, retirement contributions, building long-term wealth.
25%
Lifestyle
Dining, subscriptions, hobbies, entertainment — your "fun" money.
10%
Savings
Emergency fund, short-term goals, rainy day reserves.
💡 Paying off debt? Redirect the Growth (15%) bucket toward your debt payments instead of investing. Once your debt is cleared, shift it back to building wealth. The percentages are a starting point — adjust them in the Income & Budget tab to fit your situation.
